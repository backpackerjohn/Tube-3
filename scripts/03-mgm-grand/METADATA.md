## 2. Description (178 words · line 1 = human-narrated statement, per brand §4.2)

```
Researched, written, and narrated by a human.

The MGM Grand fire, November 21, 1980: the flames never left the casino level
of the largest hotel in the world — yet 85 people died, most of them found on
the 16th through 26th floors, killed by smoke and carbon monoxide. This
episode is reconstructed from the Clark County Fire Department and NFPA
investigation reports: the ungrounded refrigeration wire behind a closed
deli, the legal exemption that left the casino unsprinklered, flashover
crossing the casino at 15 to 19 feet per second, and the four paths — unsealed
seismic joints, elevator shafts, stairwells, and smoke dampers that could not
close — that carried smoke into rooms 25 floors above the fire. Then what
changed: Nevada's 1981 retrofit law, the rebuilt MGM's 30,000 sprinklers, and
the national fire-code reform that still protects every high-rise you sleep in.

SOURCES
```
