## 2. Description (178 words · line 1 = human-narrated statement, per brand §4.2)

```
Researched, written, and narrated by a human.

Lac-Mégantic, Québec, July 6, 2013: an unattended runaway train — 72 tank cars
of Bakken crude oil, secured by seven hand brakes and a test that couldn't
fail — rolled seven miles downhill and derailed at 65 mph in the middle of
town. Forty-seven people died, in Canada's deadliest rail disaster since 1864.
Reconstructed from Transportation Safety Board of Canada report R13D0054,
this episode covers the three braking systems and why the securement test was
meaningless with the air brakes left on; the locomotive fire that started with
a cheap engine repair; the defective valve that silently erased one of the
seven hand brakes; the Class 111 tank cars and the misclassified crude; and
the eighteen causes and contributing factors the TSB counted across one man,
one company, and one regulator — plus everything that changed afterward.

SOURCES
Official report (TSB R13D0054): https://www.tsb.gc.ca/eng/rapports-reports/rail/2013/r13d0054/r13d0054.html

Full source list in the pinned comment.
```
