## 2. Channel trailer script — "Read the Report"

**Target: 82 seconds.** Narration ≈ 178 words at the house pace (~130 wpm — calm register, deliberate). Owner-recorded voice (gate 1). Music: single sparse pulse bed, no risers, no braams; -18 LUFS under narration. Every asset is rights-safe by construction: self-drawn diagrams (rights-clean, gate 3), U.S. federal accident-report pages and figures (public domain as U.S. government works), and archival photos only from the gate-3 repositories (NARA / Library of Congress / license-verified Wikimedia Commons), each logged in the trailer's asset manifest with provenance before the edit locks. No agency (Getty/Mirrorpix/AP) imagery. No photorealistic AI generation; all motion is camera moves over stills and animation of our own diagram linework (static archival photos are never altered/animated in ways that change what the event appears to be — gate 3 / probe Task 4 tripwire).
