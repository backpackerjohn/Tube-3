# Consensus Scoring Matrix — Faceless YouTube Channel Tournament (Phase 2 Synthesis)

**Date:** 2026-07-12 · **Field:** 28 candidates (C01–C28) across 7 lanes · **Judges:** 3 lenses — Demand Skeptic, New-Entrant Realist (Attainability), Business Operator

## Methodology

- Each judge scored all 28 candidates on six criteria (1–10). Consensus per-criterion score = **mean of the three judges**, shown to 1 decimal.
- Weighted total uses the weights all three judges applied (reverse-verified against every reported per-judge total): **search demand .20, competition/attainability .20, growth .15, monetization .20, production simplicity .10, sustainability .15**. Consensus total = mean of the three judges' weighted totals (equivalent to weighting the unrounded criterion means).
- Gate flags are consolidated from all judges; no candidate hard-failed a gate (faceless: all pass; compilation: none by design), but six carry WARN-level policy exposure.
- Ranking judgment call per brief: candidates **robust across all three lenses** are preferred over spiky ones with a high mean but one collapsed lens (spread between the highest and lowest judge total is reported as "Judge spread").

## Judge Disagreement Analysis (criterion divergence > 2 points)

The three lenses agreed remarkably tightly — 22 of 28 candidates had no criterion diverging by more than 2 points, and the rank-order correlation across judges is high. Six divergences matter:

1. **C03 Agentic Dev Weekly — competition Δ4 (Demand 5 / Attainability 3 / Business 7).** The single largest disagreement in the tournament. The business judge sees room because monetization is elite and cadence is fast; the attainability judge sees the most crowded, fastest-saturating lane in the field with Anthropic itself as a competitor and version-churn deleting the back catalog. Interpretation: this is a *treadmill business* dispute, not an evidence dispute — all three agree sustainability is a 3. The attainability read should govern for a cold-start solo creator.
2. **C17 Salvage & Sinking Forensics — competition Δ3 (8 / 5 / 8).** The most consequential disagreement because it's *factual*, not philosophical: the demand and business judges accepted Waterline Salvage (created 2025-11, 831k views/14 videos) as cold-start proof; the attainability judge identified it as the incumbent Waterline network's own cross-promoted spinoff — which, if true, removes the only new-entrant evidence in the niche. This is exactly what Phase 3 must adjudicate before C17 could re-enter finalist consideration.
3. **C13 Paycheck Nation — competition Δ3 (8 / 5 / 8).** Not a factual dispute — all three judges accept the Edwards Economics proof as the strongest young-channel evidence in the field. The attainability judge is pricing in the *guaranteed fast-follower field* (the template has been publicly visible for 16 months). Both things can be true: strongest proof, closing window. Phase 3 must measure how far the window has already closed.
4. **C25 Everyday English — competition Δ3 (8 / 5 / 7).** Attainability judge prices in copycat crowding triggered by LEP's visible success plus policy-pattern exposure; the others score the demonstrated multi-channel breakouts. Moot for the shortlist given the 4.0 monetization consensus.
5. **C12 Sleep Historian — competition Δ3 (7 / 5 / 8).** Judges disagree on whether the slop flood is beatable competition (business: quality gap = opportunity) or a discovery environment where quality is invisible pre-click (attainability). All three flagged the same uninsurable risk: YouTube itself is the counterparty in this genre.
6. **C27 Aging Well — demand Δ3 (4 / 7 / 7).** The demand judge scored the *addressable* demand (its own API pull shows honest channels at 221/438 subs while misinfo farms take the 3.3M views); the others scored raw topic demand. The demand judge's read is the correct one — this is proven negative attainability regardless.

**Systemic patterns:** (a) The attainability judge was the strictest on 26 of 28 candidates — its scores are the binding constraint and are weighted accordingly in the finalist calls. (b) The gaming lane is structurally handicapped by RPM (median $3.50 vs $12–35 finance per the meta file), independent of evidence quality — all four gaming candidates land in the bottom nine. (c) Owner-interest lanes (AI, gaming) received no affinity premium from any judge; history/money/disaster won on economics and evidence.
