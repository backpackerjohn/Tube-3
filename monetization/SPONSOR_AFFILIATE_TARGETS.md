# Sponsor & Affiliate Targets — Mode of Failure

**Phase 10 deliverable · 2026-07-13 · Companion to `monetization/MONETIZATION_ROADMAP.md` (§d sequencing: affiliates day 0 · inbound sponsors from ~7k subs · active outreach at 10k subs).**
Audience fit is argued against the three personas in `brand/POSITIONING.md` §b: **P1 Mechanism Seeker** (engineers/STEM), **P2 True-Story Completionist** (documentary audience), **P3 Domain Professional** (mariners, plant operators, structural engineers). Evidence grades follow the SOURCE_LOG convention (A = primary/official/API-verified · B = reputable journalism · C = trade/creator/vendor · D = SEO-farm, caution). New URLs fetched for this deliverable are cited inline with grades.

**Standing suitability screen (applies to every deal):** no fear-bait ad scripts (breaks the calm register, BRAND §2), no products that trade on tragedy, no reads on recent-event episodes (Surfside, Key Bridge class), ≤1 read per 2 videos at 60–90s post-cold-open, and the read is written in-house in channel voice. FTC disclosure on every affiliate link and read. All programs below cost $0 to join (D-005 budget intact).

## Tier A — Engineering-education class (the memo's designated sponsor surface)

Memo v2 thesis §2: the forensic spine "widens the sponsor surface toward the proven engineering-channel sponsor set (Brilliant/CuriosityStream/Nebula class) rather than the unproven VPN/audiobook claim struck in correction #5." These are the highest-fit targets.

### 1. Brilliant (brilliant.org) — interactive STEM courses
- **Fit:** P1 dead-center — a viewer who just watched an eyebar fracture-mechanics breakdown is the exact "learn the underlying math/physics" conversion Brilliant buys. P3 secondary (professional upskilling).
- **Evidence they sponsor similar channels:** first-party sponsor landing page for Real Engineering — https://brilliant.org/landing/sponsor/realengineering/ **[A — the brand's own co-branded page]**; Real Engineering's founder publicly discusses producing Brilliant-sponsored videos — https://x.com/TheBrianMcManus/status/1813780638794686751 **[B — creator primary]**; ThoughtLeaders case study documents Brilliant's YouTube-first sponsorship strategy (post-roll placements, "20% off for the first 200" offer structure) — https://www.thoughtleaders.io/blog/case-study-brilliant **[C]**.
- **Entry point:** affiliate program exists (~$25/sale reported — https://getlasso.co/affiliate/brilliant/ **[C]**), joinable pre-scale. Direct sponsorship realistically at **50k+ subs** (they buy educational reach); pitch at 10k only with a breakout video (>250k views) as the proof asset.
- **Outreach hook (see template):** "Our median viewer just sat through 15 minutes of fracture mechanics voluntarily."

### 2. CuriosityStream — documentary streaming
- **Fit:** P2 dead-center (documentary completionists are their subscriber profile); P1 secondary.
- **Evidence:** long history of sponsoring engineering/history YouTube (the CuriosityStream+Nebula bundle was *the* educational-YouTube sponsorship vehicle 2020–2024 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nebula_(streaming_service) **[B]**). **Caveat, honestly stated:** the Nebula bundle ended Dec 31, 2024, and their creator-sponsorship spend has visibly contracted since — treat direct-deal likelihood as reduced from its 2021 peak.
- **Entry point:** **affiliate at 0 subs** — program live on FlexOffers with ~$5–20/signup terms and a 45-day cookie (https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/curiositystream-affiliate-program/ **[B-]**; https://getlasso.co/affiliate/curiositystream/ **[C]**). Direct sponsorship pitch at 25k+.

### 3. Nebula — creator-owned streaming
- **Fit:** P1/P2; the platform's catalog (Real Engineering, Wendover, RealLifeLore — Wikipedia above **[B]**) is our audience's existing diet.
- **Evidence:** Nebula isn't a conventional sponsor — it's an invite-based creator co-op (revenue share + equity-like participation).
- **Entry point:** **long-game target, not an outreach target.** Realistic only after sustained six-figure-view performance (~100k+ subs). Log it as the aspirational distribution/revenue leg; do not email them at 10k.

### 4. Ground News — news-comparison app
- **Fit:** strong and unusually on-brand: the channel's banner line is *"We start where the news stopped and the report began"* (POSITIONING §c) — a media-literacy product is a natural read that can be scripted in the house register without fear-bait. P1/P2/P3 all plausible.
- **Evidence:** the #1 YouTube sponsor by volume in H1 2025 — 1,863 sponsored integrations, +202% YoY, 664M sponsored-video views (https://www.axios.com/2025/10/22/youtube-sponsorship-creator-videos **[B — Axios]**); 423+ channels tracked (https://sponsorradar.com/brands/ground-news **[C]**). A brand buying at that volume buys mid-sized channels.
- **Entry point:** **affiliate at 0 subs** — first-party program at https://ground.news/affiliates **[A — first-party]**. Direct read pitch viable from ~10k subs given their volume strategy.

### 5. KiwiCo — STEM project crates
- **Fit:** P1 (engineers with kids is a large overlap demographic **[inference — no first-party demo data]**); the "how things fail" → "build things that work" pairing reads naturally.
- **Evidence:** ubiquitous sponsor of the engineering-education YouTube class (Mark Rober tier and below) — **[C — widely observable in-format, not independently fetched for this file; flagged accordingly]**.
- **Entry point:** direct/influencer platform at **10k subs**; no meaningful affiliate path for our format below that.

### 6. Autodesk Fusion / Onshape (PTC) — CAD software trials
- **Fit:** P1/P3 precisely — our diagrams are drafted in CAD-adjacent tooling and every failure episode is implicitly a design-review lesson. A CAD trial read is native content.
- **Evidence:** **[inference, flagged]** — engineering YouTube carries CAD sponsorships regularly, but no specific fetched proof for this file. Verify via SponsorRadar/ThoughtLeaders before pitching.
- **Entry point:** direct at **10–25k subs**, education/developer-marketing teams; both companies run education outreach programs that answer cold email.

### 7. Ansys / simulation-software education arms
- **Fit:** P1/P3 — FEA/simulation is literally the tool that models the failures we narrate ("here's the stress concentration the 1967 engineers couldn't compute").
- **Evidence:** **[inference, flagged]** — Ansys has sponsored large engineering channels historically; not independently fetched here. Verify before pitching.
- **Entry point:** direct at **25k+ subs**; earlier, their free student-license program can be referenced editorially (no deal needed) to warm the relationship.

## Tier B — Safety / professional class

### 8. MIRA Safety — PPE / CBRN respirators
- **Fit:** P3 strong (plant operators, industrial safety), P1 moderate. Product category is adjacent to our subject matter without trading on tragedy — a respirator read after a Texas City episode is contextual, not ghoulish, **if scripted to the "the fix" register** (BRAND rule 10).
- **Evidence:** runs an active affiliate/sponsorship program with named values-based partner screening (https://www.mirasafety.com/blogs/news/mira-origin-story **[A — first-party re: program existence]**); heavy presence in preparedness/disaster-adjacent YouTube **[C — observable, not fetched]**.
- **Entry point:** affiliate joinable early; direct read from ~10k. **Two-way screening caveat:** the brand publicly screens partners on its own values criteria — review fit both directions before signing.
- **Suitability note:** never run a PPE read on an episode where the depicted victims died for lack of that PPE — that crosses the no-trading-on-tragedy line. Schedule reads on diagram-showcase episodes instead.

### 9. Industrial-supply & PPE retail affiliates (Amazon Associates basket; Zoro/Grainger via affiliate networks)
- **Fit:** P3/P1 — "further reading + gear" description block: the NTSB report in print, engineering-failure books (Petroski's *To Engineer Is Human*), hard hats/respirators/hearing protection.
- **Evidence:** Amazon Associates is format-agnostic and proven for description-link monetization generally **[A — program terms public]**; census precedent that description links work in-lane (FH's 100/100 Patreon links, census Q3).
- **Entry point:** **0 subs, day 1.** Note Amazon's rule: ≥3 qualifying sales within 180 days or the account closes — re-apply later if it lapses; cost is $0 either way.

### 10. Skillshare / professional-learning platforms
- **Fit:** P1 secondary (career-skills adjacency); weaker than Brilliant because the content link is generic.
- **Evidence:** long-running mass sponsor of educational YouTube **[C — widely observable; not fetched]**; affiliate via impact.com-class networks (https://impact.com/ **[B — network exists; specific program terms unverified]**).
- **Entry point:** affiliate at 0 subs; direct read at 10–25k. Rank below Ground News/Brilliant in outreach priority — fit is adequate, not distinctive.

## Tier D — Census-contradicted for this lane (include only with this caveat)

### 13. VPNs (NordVPN / Surfshark class)
- **CAVEAT FIRST:** memo v2 correction #5 struck the claim that VPN sponsors are standard in this format — **no VPN reads were observed in FH's last 100 videos**, and the NordVPN evidence belonged to a different lane's channel (Dr. Tam's sociology-explainer channel, probe Task 2). Do not model VPN money into any projection.
- **Why still listed:** VPNs remain the highest-volume sponsor class on YouTube overall, and they demonstrably buy *faceless narrator formats* (NordVPN slug on Dr. Tam **[A]**) — so an inbound offer is plausible and acceptable if the read passes the no-fear-bait screen.
- **Entry point:** inbound only; do not spend outreach effort here.

### 14. Audiobooks (Audible class)
- **CAVEAT FIRST:** same correction #5 — no audiobook reads observed in the in-lane sample. Census-contradicted as a *reliable* buyer of this inventory.
- **Why still listed:** topical fit is real (disaster nonfiction is a large audiobook genre; a *Fascinating-Horror-adjacent* listener overlap is plausible **[inference]**), and Amazon's Audible affiliate (bounty ~$5–10/trial, program terms public **[A]**) is joinable at 0 subs — so it costs nothing to carry the link.
- **Entry point:** affiliate only until the channel has its own conversion data; no outreach.

### 15. Established-audience documentary streamers (MagellanTV)
- **Fit:** P2 direct (documentary streamer with a large disaster/engineering catalog).
- **Evidence:** heavy, long-running sponsor of history/documentary YouTube **[C — widely observable; not independently fetched for this file — verify via SponsorRadar before pitching]**; runs an open affiliate program **[C]**.
- **Entry point:** affiliate early; direct pitch at 10–25k subs. Treated as Tier D only for evidence grade, not fit — upgrade to Tier A if verification lands.
